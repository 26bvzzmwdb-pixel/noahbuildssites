What does it cost?

$300 one-time setup, due only after you approve the finished site, then $40/month for hosting, domain renewal, security updates and up to 2 small edits a month.

Do I have to pay anything for the mockup?

No. The mockup is free and there's no obligation. If you don't love it, you don't owe anything.

Facebook is great for posts, but people searching on Google want your hours, services, location and a way to call in seconds. A simple website gives them that, and it's yours, not Facebook's.

You do. Your domain and your content are yours.
